Virat Kohli

This is a simple webpage about Virat Kohli.

Virat Kohli (born 5 November 1988) is an Indian international cricketer and the former all-format captain of the Indian national cricket team. He is a right-handed batter and occasional right-arm medium pace bowler. Considered one of the greatest all-format batsmen in the history of cricket, he has been acclaimed for his batting skills, records and ability to lead his team to victory. Kohli has the most centuries in ODIs and the second-most centuries in international cricket with 85 tons across all formats. He is also the leading run-scorer in the Indian Premier League. Kohli is the most successful Test captain of India with most wins and 3 consecutive Test mace retainments. He is the only batter to earn 900+ rating points across all 3 formats.

Current Format Status & 2027 World Cup Goal

Following a legendary international career spanning over 15 years, Kohli has carefully streamlined his commitments:

    ● T20 Internationals: He officially retired from T20Is immediately after winning the 2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup, where he was named Player of the Match in the final.
    ● ODIs: He continues to play ODIs and is aiming to participate in the 2027 World Cup.
    ● Test Matches: He has retired from Test cricket to focus on limited-overs formats and prolong his career.
    ● Indian Premier League (IPL): He remains an active player in the IPL, leading Royal Challengers Bangalore and contributing significantly to their campaigns.
